### FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**Teed Up Launches: The First App to Turn Golf Booking Screenshots into Calendar Events Automatically**

*Dubai, UAE — June 2026*

The Artesian Group® today announced the launch of **Teed Up**, a mobile app for iOS and Android that eliminates the tedious process of organising golf groups by scanning booking confirmation screenshots and automatically creating calendar events with player invitations.

**The Problem**

Every week, millions of golfers follow the same routine: screenshot their booking confirmation, manually type the details into a group chat, chase multiple people for responses, and then separately create a calendar event. It's a 5–10 minute process that happens every single time.

**The Solution**

Teed Up uses on-device AI to read any booking confirmation screenshot — from any golf course, any booking platform, anywhere in the world. In under 10 seconds, the app extracts the course name, date, time, and number of players, then creates a calendar event and sends invitations to selected playing partners.

**Key Features:**
- Scans any booking screenshot using on-device OCR
- Creates calendar events compatible with iCloud, Google Calendar, Outlook, and Exchange
- Invites playing partners directly from device contacts
- Sends instant decline alerts when a player drops out
- Smart amendments that only notify new players
- 100% on-device processing — no data ever leaves the user's phone

**Privacy-First Architecture**

Unlike competing apps that require account creation and cloud synchronisation, Teed Up operates entirely on-device. No user data is collected, transmitted, or stored externally. The app requires no account creation — users simply grant calendar, contacts, and camera permissions and start scanning.

**Pricing**

Teed Up is available as a one-time purchase for AED 99 (approximately USD $27.99) with no subscription fees, no in-app purchases, and no advertising. The price is deliberately positioned below the cost of a single round of golf at most courses.

**Availability**

Teed Up is available now on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

**About The Artesian Group®**

The Artesian Group® is a Dubai-based technology company specialising in AI-powered productivity tools. For more information, visit [theartesiangroup.com](https://theartesiangroup.com).
