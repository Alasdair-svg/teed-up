Snap your booking. Your group never misses a tee time.

All Teed Up turns a screenshot of your golf booking confirmation into a shared calendar event in seconds — no typing, no manual entry.

HOW IT WORKS
Snap or import a screenshot of your tee time confirmation (from Viya, your club's app, WhatsApp — any format). All Teed Up reads the course, date, tee time, and player names on your device and builds the round for you.

STAY IN SYNC
Every player gets added to your device calendar automatically, with reminders before the round. RSVP status updates for the whole group so you always know who's actually showing up.

LET FRIENDS AND FAMILY KNOW
Add your regular playing partners once, then notify them with a tap — they get the details without needing the app themselves.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
All Teed Up runs entirely on your phone. Text recognition happens on-device — no accounts, no cloud, no servers. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is the calendar invite you choose to send.

FEATURES
• On-device screenshot scanning — works with any booking format
• Automatic calendar sync with reminders
• Player RSVP tracking (confirmed, pending, declined)
• Friends & family notifications
• No sign-up, no account, no ads

Built for golfers who'd rather be on the course than juggling group chats and calendar invites.
