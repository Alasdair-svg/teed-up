Declines now reach you. Three faults were stopping them:

• The app stopped watching a round the moment its tee time passed — exactly
when people drop out. It now watches 14 days either side.

• Rounds were only watched if their calendar was the one picked in Settings.
The app now finds a round by the calendar it was saved to, or by its golf
marker in any calendar on your phone.

• Alerts were written to one store and read from another, so a decline found
in the background was never shown.

Rounds already played now move to their own section.
