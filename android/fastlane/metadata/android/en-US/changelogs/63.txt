Fixes from beta feedback:

• Declines and acceptances are now detected. The app was reading RSVPs from
your calendar every 60 seconds and discarding every one, so no alert ever
fired. Rounds you already have repair themselves — no reinstall needed.

• Rescanning an amended booking now reaches the calendar. A player who
joined gets an invite, one who dropped out gets a cancellation, and everyone
still playing gets an updated event saying who changed.
