What All Teed Up Does

All Teed Up turns a screenshot of your golf booking confirmation into a shared calendar event — automatically. Snap or import a screenshot, and the App reads the course, date, tee time, and player names on your device, then builds the round for you with reminders and RSVP tracking.

Everything runs on your phone — no accounts, no sign-up, no cloud backend.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the App need Camera / Photos access?

To scan the screenshot of your booking confirmation. Text recognition runs entirely on-device — the image is never uploaded anywhere.

Why does the App need Contacts access?

To match player names on your booking to their email addresses, so calendar invites can be addressed correctly. This lookup happens entirely on-device.

Why does the App need Calendar access?

To create the round as an event in your device's native calendar (iCloud, Google, Outlook, or Exchange) and keep reminders and RSVPs in sync.

The scan didn't pick up the right details — what do I do?

Booking screenshots come in a lot of formats. If a course, time, or player is missed or wrong, you can correct it directly on the review screen before the round is saved — nothing is created until you confirm.

How do I remove my data?

Uninstalling the App deletes everything stored on your device. Calendar events already created remain in your device calendar unless you delete them separately, since they're managed by your calendar provider, not the App.
